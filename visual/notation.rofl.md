# The notation view

> A picture the domain draws itself: the notebook's facts written in the
> domain's own standard and handed to its engine, as docs/renderers.md says
> of notations (a pedigree, a formula, a score). The first is GEDCOM 7, the
> standard family trees are exchanged in: a person with a name and a birth
> year, a family of partners and children. `draw notation` writes the file;
> VS Code opens it beside the notebook for the genealogy tool of your choice.

Declared as facts:

- <a id="named"></a>A person P is named S
- <a id="born"></a>A person P was born in Y
- <a id="partner"></a>A family F has the partner P
- <a id="child"></a>A family F has the child C
