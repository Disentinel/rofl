# ROFL 1.9 — release notes (unreleased)

Draft on branch `claude/project-thread-lii3ye`, PR #307: the engine on pages.

## What an agent must do differently

1. **Nothing in the verbs.** `rofl ask`, `why`, `whynot`, `excise`, `rofl load`, `rofl serve` and kept worlds take
   the same input and give the same answers. The JS worlds of `npm test` answer as on main.
2. **The levels are gone.** A corpus is evaluated whole on one heap. The knobs `ROFL_VOLUMES`, `ROFL_FAN`,
   `ROFL_LEAF`, `ROFL_HELD`, `ROFL_FAR`, `ROFL_WIRE_*` and `ROFL_CENSUS` are gone with them, and so is the federation
   by levels between processes. `rofl serve`'s `volumes` and `volumes_edit` stay: the corpus is evaluated whole and
   an edit is taken by delta.
3. **Walls stop in other places.** A step or disk wall that falls in a pages pass resumes from the component it
   stopped in. A world whose meaning is the stop by budget has a new golden.

## The engine on pages

A pass whose rules the core takes (`engine/pages.rs`) files each fact under every key a premise asks it by. The
components of relations settle lowest first, each by its news. Firings without side effects are solved on threads.
A pass the core does not take goes to the rounds with the same answers: ticks, lattices, thresholds, demand,
aliases, provenance readers, and worlds planned by their profile.

Release build, one machine (4 cores); the memory column remeasured after the allocator change, on a machine then
slower, so the times are the earlier run's, the JS model's `calls`, main's model (#301) on both; every answer equal to
601eb3e's where both answer.

## Less memory resident

- **What is freed goes back to the system after 10 ms**, mimalloc v2's delay instead of v3's second: held a second,
  it was a quarter of axios's resident memory. A small world is now below 601eb3e's (axios 321 MB against 350).
- **An index of the pages keeps a fact's id and half its key**, 8 bytes a slot instead of 16, the key said again
  from the fact when the index grows (eslint/lib: the pages 146 -> 92 MB).
- **`peak:` on stderr names the parts at the true peak**, the pages among them.

## A world larger than memory

- **The memory wall cools the world to disk and it goes on** (`Session::cool_and_go_on`, docs/cli.md). hono in a
  2.5 GB cgroup was partial; now it is cooled once and whole, its answer equal to a run with room.
- **A large load is read a block at a time.** vscode/src/vs/platform (18.6 M facts) peaked at 10 GB in the load
  and was killed one run in two; now it peaks at 3.7 GB, and its first step comes at 28 s instead of 45 s.
- **A world on pages is kept whatever its volume.** The 3x estimate of the levels path is gone, and the next
  question over hono is answered in under a second.

## Known gaps

- vscode/src/vs/platform (3 097 files) does not answer in 10 GB. It is cooled twice (33 M steps each), then the
  pages index of the pass outgrows what a cooling frees; it stops partial and says so.
- The JS model was not measured on the whole of VS Code.
- The memory of *Less memory resident* cost eslint/lib about 5% of its time and axios 2% (A/B on the fast build,
  against 3c24bd5, whose times the table gives).
