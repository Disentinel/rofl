## What bit

**Normalization must be total or it silently splits the world.**
Canonicalizing obligations alone is a trap: evidence recorded against an
alias would then support nothing, and a raw `blocking(...)` query in the
scheduler would mis-rank canonical targets. Three touchpoints had to move
together: obligations (`requires_on`/`blocks_on`), epistemic evidence
routing (`canonical` in supports/refutes), and the scheduler's ranking
query. Recorded as `f_normalization_must_be_total` (settled) — any future
relation that takes a claim argument must decide alias-or-canonical
explicitly.
